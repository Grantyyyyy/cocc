# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Iligan City deciding whether to visit Crossed Over Citi Church (COCC) for the first time, plus members checking service details. Inferred from the brief: most arrive from Facebook on a phone.

## Product Purpose

A single landing page whose job is to get a visitor to attend Sunday Service. Success is a visitor knowing when and where to come, feeling welcome, and acting (planning a visit or watching online).

## Positioning

COCC meets on Sundays at Plaza Alemania, 2nd Floor, in Iligan City, and welcomes people in Cebuano and English: "Kung kinsa man ka, unsa man ka, welcome ka!" (Whoever you are, whatever you are, you are welcome.)

## Operating Context

- Sunday Service, 9AM to 11AM, Plaza Alemania, 2nd Floor, Iligan City (venue updated per the user; the time is from the church banner).
- Public channel: facebook.com/cocciligan.
- The church already publishes designed banners with scripture (e.g. Psalm 65:11) and a "JOIN US" call to action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind v4. Content lives in `src/data/site.ts` and `src/data/content.ts`.
- The visit form has no backend yet; it is a labeled demo.
- Confirmed by the user: phone (063) 224 0407, email coccyan@gmail.com, office at Crossed Over Center, 2nd Floor, Plaza Alemania, Iligan City.
- Undecided: other weekly meetings, ministries, events, leader names and roles, domain. Current values for these are placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Crossed Over Citi Church, short form COCC.
- Logo: gold COCC monogram shaped as a fish, on violet (`public/brand/cocc-logo.webp`; the PNG app icon `src/app/icon.png` feeds the social share image).
- Colors: violet `#8214D9`, gold `#FCDC58`, white.
- Tagline: "Building lives. Glorifying God."
- Welcome line: "Kung kinsa man ka, unsa man ka, welcome ka!"
- Voice: warm, direct, bilingual (Cebuano and English).
- Copy uses no em dashes (user instruction).
- Visual reference the user supplied: the Tony Robbins homepage (dark page, inset hero card, poster carousel, full-bleed photo band, stacked pillar words).

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo artwork (PNG, 2048px square, violet background).
- A Sunday Service banner featuring two church leaders and the congregation; it has text baked into the image, so it cannot be cropped into clean photography.
- Absent, and must not be fabricated as real: photos without text, leader names, testimonials, attendance figures, event dates.

## Product Principles

1. Welcome before information: the first thing a visitor meets is that they belong.
2. One action: every section points toward coming on Sunday.
3. Honest content: placeholders stay labeled until the church confirms them.
4. Speak like the church speaks: Cebuano warmth alongside English clarity.
